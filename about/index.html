About

Small tools, made properly.

Futzs builds productivity apps for Google Workspace. The premise is simple: most of the time people lose at work goes to small, repeated friction — a calendar that does not match another calendar, the same reply typed for the fiftieth time, an on-call rota that lives in a spreadsheet. Each of our apps takes one of those and removes it.

We work in the open where we can. GAPPS, the Python library we use to build these add-ons, is free for anyone to use, and the same care goes into the paid apps as into the library underneath them.

If a tool you need does not exist yet, we also build custom internal Workspace tools for teams — reporting, billing, stock management, CRM workflows, whatever the day actually looks like.
