Questions

Before you install

Does DutyDesk read our messages?

No. A Chat app only receives the messages that mention it, the commands sent to it and the buttons pressed on its cards. It acts as an app with the chat.bot permission and never asks for access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or other Google data. The side panel only learns who opened it.

Who sees the request cards?

Request and handoff cards are posted in the space, so its members see them like any message. Your /dd_duty card, plans and confirmations are private.

What if nobody claims a request?

After 30 minutes DutyDesk mentions the person on duty again in the thread; after an hour the request goes to the next person in the rotation, once. Change the delay or turn it off in /dd_setup.

What about holidays?

Enter the first and last day away from /dd_duty, for yourself or a teammate. DutyDesk passes that person over at every handoff that touches those days. For a few hours or days, Cover a shift sends requests to someone else without changing the rotation.

Can I see the rotation in Google Calendar?

Yes: /dd_duty › Add to calendar gives a private link to subscribe to in Google Calendar. Anyone with the link sees the names in the rotation; New link turns the old one off.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every space gets 21 days with the Team limits, no card needed. DutyDesk reminds the space before the trial ends.

What happens when the trial ends without a plan?

Nothing is deleted. The space becomes read only, /dd_duty still answers, but new requests are not routed and handoffs are not posted until the space subscribes.

Is it an incident or emergency tool?

DutyDesk routes requests and notifies the person on duty through Google Chat. It is not a monitoring or emergency service, so keep your phone tree for real emergencies.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil), German, Italian and Japanese. A new space starts in the language of whoever adds DutyDesk; /dd_setup changes it.

How do I cancel?

/dd_help › See plans › Manage subscription, or the link in Polar's receipt. The plan stays active until the end of the period already paid, and payments are refunded in full within 14 days.
