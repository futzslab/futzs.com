DutyDesk

@DutyDesk in Google Chat reaches whoever is on duty right now.

An on-call rotation that lives in your Google Chat space. Mention @DutyDesk, and the request goes to the person on duty, with buttons to claim and resolve it.

DutyDesk screenshot

One handle for the whole rotation

Google Chat has no group mention. @DutyDesk stands in for whoever is on duty, so nobody has to check a spreadsheet first.

Handoffs on schedule

Daily or weekly rotations in your team's timezone. DutyDesk posts the handoff in the space and names who is next.

Priced for small teams

One flat price per space, not per seat.

Get started

How it works

01

Add DutyDesk to a space

The space your team already uses for requests.

02

Run /dd_setup

Pick the people, the cadence and the handoff time.

03

Mention @DutyDesk

The request goes to the person on duty, who can claim it, hand it on or resolve it from the card.
