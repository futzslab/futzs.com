Questions

Before you install

Where do my templates live?

They stay attached to your account and are available wherever you compose. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is stored.

Can I use it with a personal Gmail account?

Yes — install it for yourself from the Marketplace, no administrator needed.

Can my team share replies?

A Workspace administrator can install the app for the whole domain so everyone works from the same setup.

What happens if I uninstall?

Access is revoked immediately from the Marketplace or your Google account's connected apps.
