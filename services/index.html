Services

Scientific software services.

Alongside our apps, Futzs helps R&D teams build, speed up and maintain the scientific software they depend on. The work is led by Serge Koudoro, maintainer of the open-source libraries DIPY, FURY and polyxios.

Pipelines

Custom imaging & data pipelines

Diffusion MRI, microscopy/FLIM and 3D mesh pipelines, reproducible, tested and documented so your team can own them.

Performance

Performance optimization

Profiling and rewriting the slow parts of scientific code in Cython, SIMD or GPU, with benchmarks before and after on your data.

Formats

File formats & data conversion

NIfTI, DICOM, mesh and proprietary formats, plus migration from meshio to polyxios, validated on your files.

Support

Maintenance & support contracts

Priority fixes, code review and long-term support for the internal tools your team relies on.

How we work together

  • Audit: a fixed-price review of your pipeline or codebase, with a written report and a prioritized plan.
  • Sprint: one or two weeks on a well-defined goal, at a fixed scope and price.
  • Retainer: a monthly block of hours for ongoing development, support and code review.

Who you work with

Serge Koudoro has 15+ years of experience building scientific software, including 7 years of industrial R&D. He is the lead maintainer and release manager of DIPY, release manager of FURY and creator of polyxios, and a patent holder in multiphoton imaging. Trusted by industrial R&D teams.

Case studies, background and FAQ: sergekoudoro.com/services.
