DutyDesk — Privacy & Terms

What the Chat app reads and stores, who sees it, the terms of use, and billing and refunds.

Privacy Policy

In Short

  • DutyDesk is a Google Chat app. It acts as itself (an app with the chat.bot permission), never as you: it does not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or any other Google data.
  • It only sees the messages that mention @DutyDesk, the commands you send it, the buttons you press on its cards, and what you do in its side panel. It does not read the other messages of the space, nor your emails, events or files.
  • What we store, per Chat space: the space name, the rotation (people, order, schedule, timezone), who was on duty when, and for each request the first 200 characters of its text, who asked, who it was assigned to and its status.
  • We do not sell your data or share it, except with Polar (polar.sh), which processes payments and receives the buyer’s e-mail address for that purpose, and PostHog, which receives product usage events about a space under a random identifier and side-panel use under a one-way hash of the person (never message text, names, e-mail addresses or Google account IDs).
  • The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google permissions

Permission Why DutyDesk asks for it

How DutyDesk uses this data

DutyDesk uses the data above only to provide its features to the space: keep the rotation, post handoffs, route and track requests, and bill the space. We never:

  • use it for advertising, including targeted, personalized, retargeted or interest-based advertising;
  • sell it, or give it to data brokers or information resellers;
  • use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending;
  • use it to build databases beyond what the features above need;
  • use it, or any data from Google Workspace APIs, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or ML models.

Who sees what

Rotation, handoff and request cards are posted in the space, so every member of the space sees them, as they see any message. Cards about one person (your duty, help, plans, confirmations) are private: only that person sees them. The calendar feed link shows the display names and shifts of the rotation to anyone who has the link; New link turns the old link off.

Security

Data is stored in our own database, not shared with other Futzs apps. All traffic to and from DutyDesk is encrypted (HTTPS). Every request from Google Chat is checked to come from Google before DutyDesk acts on it. The database is on a server only the Futzs team can access, and used only to support the service. DutyDesk’s Google service account key and payment webhook secret are stored as server secrets, never in the code.

How long DutyDesk keeps data

DutyDesk keeps a space’s rotation and requests while it is installed in the space. When the app is removed, the data is kept so the rotation comes back as it was if the app is added again. To erase everything DutyDesk stores about a space, a member of that space can e-mail support-dutydesk@futzs.com; it is done within 30 days. Payment records kept by Polar follow Polar’s own retention rules.
