Quick Replies — Privacy & Terms

Google permissions, what is stored, for how long, and the terms of use.

Privacy Policy

In Short

  • Quick Replies stores the replies you create (a name and the message text), your settings, your e-mail address, and the Google authorization that lets it send a reply as you in Google Chat.
  • It sends a message as you only when you ask it to, with a command or a button.
  • It does not read your other messages, your Gmail, your Drive or your Calendar.
  • We do not sell your data or share it, except with Polar (polar.sh) for payments and PostHog for product usage statistics under a pseudonymous identifier (never your messages).
  • The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google permissions

Permission Why Quick Replies asks for it
chat.bot (granted to the app, not to your account) Answer your commands and show its cards in Google Chat
chat.messages.create Send the reply you picked as a message from you, in the conversation where you used it
userinfo.email Know which account your replies belong to
script.locale Your language and timezone
script.external_request, script.scriptapp Let the add-on contact the Quick Replies server and run its tasks

What Quick Replies stores

Data Why Kept until
Your e-mail address Your account You ask us to delete your account
Your replies (name and text) and settings The feature itself You delete them in Quick Replies, or delete your account
Your Google authorization (refresh token) Sending a reply as you when you ask You revoke it, or delete your account
Your plan and license Billing You delete your account; Polar keeps payment records under its own rules

How Quick Replies uses this data

Only to provide its features to you: store your replies, show them to you, and send the one you pick. We never:

  • use it for advertising, including targeted, personalized, retargeted or interest-based advertising;
  • sell it, or give it to data brokers or information resellers;
  • use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending;
  • use it, or any data from Google Workspace APIs, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or ML models.

Nobody at Futzs reads your replies, except when you ask us for help with one or to fix a problem that affects it.

Who receives your data

Who Why What
Google Chat Where your replies are sent The message you chose to send
Polar Software Inc. (polar.sh) Payments, as merchant of record Your e-mail address and payment details, entered on Polar’s page
PostHog Product analytics Usage events under a salted hash of your e-mail, never the e-mail itself or your messages
Supabase and Google Cloud Hosting the database and the app The data described above

We also disclose data when the law requires it, for example to answer a court order.

How Quick Replies protects your data

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). The database is not public, and access to it is limited to the Futzs team, to support the service. You can revoke Quick Replies’ access to your account at any time at myaccount.google.com › Security › Third-party apps & services; it then stops sending messages as you.

Deleting your data

Delete a reply from Quick Replies and it is gone. To delete your account and everything Quick Replies stores about you, e-mail support-quickreplies@futzs.com; it is done within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We post every change on this page with its date. If a change affects how Quick Replies uses Google user data, Google asks for your consent again before Quick Replies gets new access. Last updated: November 8, 2026.
