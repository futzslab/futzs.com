Unlinker — Privacy & Terms

What the add-on can open in Drive, what happens to your file, what is stored, the terms of use, and the license and refunds.

Privacy Policy

In Short

  • Unlinker is a Google Workspace add-on for Google Drive. It removes the links from the PDFs you select.
  • It can only open the files you select and allow, one by one, and the copies it creates. It cannot browse, list or search the rest of your Drive.
  • To clean a PDF, the file is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, processed in memory and sent straight back to your Drive. It is never written to disk, never stored and never logged: not its content, not its name, not its Drive ID.
  • What we store, per Google account: your Google account ID, your e-mail address, how many free files you used, whether you have a license, and the date and kind of each file processed (for example “pdf”). Nothing about the files themselves.
  • We do not sell your data or share it, except with Polar (polar.sh), which processes payments and receives the buyer’s e-mail address for that purpose, and PostHog, which receives product usage events under a random identifier (never a file name, file content, e-mail address or Google account ID).
  • The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your files

A PDF you clean travels from Google Drive to the add-on (which runs on Google’s servers, as you), then over HTTPS to the Unlinker server, and back the same way. On our server the file exists only in memory, for the time it takes to process it, usually a few seconds. It is not written to disk, not copied to a queue or a backup, not kept for debugging, and not used for anything other than producing the cleaned copy.

Our logs record that a request happened and whether it succeeded. They never contain a file name, text from a document, a Drive ID or a link found in a file. If processing fails, only the type of error is logged.

Your original file is never modified or deleted. Unlinker only creates a new file.

Security

Data is stored in Unlinker’s own database, not shared with other Futzs apps. All traffic to and from Unlinker is encrypted (HTTPS). Every request from the add-on carries a Google-signed identity token, which the server verifies before it does anything. The database is on a server only the Futzs team can access, and used only to support the service; its backups are encrypted. License keys are stored only as one-way hashes, and the payment webhook secret is stored as a server secret, never in the code.

How long Unlinker keeps data

Files are not kept at all. Account data (Google account ID, e-mail address, free files used, license, and the date and kind of each file processed) is kept while you use Unlinker, so your license keeps working. To erase everything Unlinker stores about you, e-mail support-unlinker@futzs.com from the address of the account; it is done within 30 days, and a license cannot be recovered afterwards. Payment records kept by Polar follow Polar’s own retention rules.

To take back Unlinker’s access to the files you allowed, uninstall it, or remove it from your Google account’s third-party access.
