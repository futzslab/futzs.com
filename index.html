Before you install

The questions we get asked

What exactly is Futzs?

Futzs builds small, focused productivity apps for Google Workspace — plus open-source libraries for developers who want to build their own. Every app installs from the Google Workspace Marketplace and works inside the tools you already use.

Do I need my Workspace admin to install an app?

Individual users can install any of our apps for themselves from the Marketplace. Domain-wide installs, where everyone in your organisation gets the app at once, are done by a Workspace administrator.

What happens to my data?

Our apps only request the Google permissions they need to do their job, and each app publishes its own privacy policy describing exactly what is accessed and stored. You can read them on the legal page.

Can you build something for my team?

Yes. We build custom internal Google Workspace tools — reporting, billing, stock management, CRM workflows and more. Get in touch and tell us what the workflow looks like today.

Where do I get help?

Start at the Help Center, or email us directly. Bugs and feature requests for the open-source projects are best filed as GitHub issues.
