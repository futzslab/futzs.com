Common questions

How do I install an app?

Every app is published on the Google Workspace Marketplace. Open the listing, choose Install, and grant the permissions the app asks for. Nothing else to set up.

Can I install for my whole organisation?

Yes — a Google Workspace administrator can install any of our apps domain-wide from the Marketplace, so everyone gets it at once.

Which permissions do the apps ask for?

Only the ones required for the app to work. Each app lists its scopes on its Marketplace listing and explains them in its privacy policy.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with the app name and what you were doing when it happened. For the open-source projects, a GitHub issue gets the fastest response.

How do I uninstall?

Remove the app from the Google Workspace Marketplace or from your Google account's connected apps. Access is revoked immediately.
