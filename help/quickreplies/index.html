Getting started

Quick Replies stores the messages you send most often in Google Chat and sends the one you pick, as you, with one command.

1. Install the app

Open the Quick Replies listing on the Google Workspace Marketplace and choose Install. In Google Chat, open any conversation and type /qrHelp.

2. Write your first reply

Type /qrNew to open the dialog, or write it in one line: /qrNew hours We are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. Pick a shortcut you will remember: short, lowercase, specific.

3. Send it with /qr

In any conversation, type /qr hours. The first time, Google asks you to allow Quick Replies to send messages as you; after that, the reply is sent right away.

Commands

Command What it does
/qr <shortcut> Send a reply
/qrNew Create a reply (dialog, or /qrNew <shortcut> <message>)
/qrList List and search your replies
/qrEdit <shortcut> Change a reply
/qrRemove <shortcut> Delete a reply
/qrHelp Show the help

Troubleshooting

Quick Replies does not answer

Type the command at the start of the message and pick it from the list Chat shows, so it is sent as a command and not as text.

“Authorization required”

Quick Replies needs your permission to send messages as you. Follow the link on the card, then send /qr again. If you revoked access in your Google account, the same link restores it.

My replies are missing in another conversation

Replies are attached to your Google account. Confirm you use Chat with the same account you created them with.
