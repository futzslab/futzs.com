Getting started

Google Chat has no way to mention a group. DutyDesk gives your team one handle, @DutyDesk, that always reaches whoever is on duty right now. It keeps the rotation, posts the handoff when the duty changes hands, and turns each mention into a request card the person on duty can claim, hand on or resolve.

Add DutyDesk to a space

  1. Open the Google Chat space your team uses for requests.
  2. Click the space name › Apps & integrations › Add apps.
  3. Search for DutyDesk and choose Add.

DutyDesk posts a short checklist in the space. It ticks itself off as you go.

Set up the rotation

Type /dd_setup in the space. In the dialog:

  • Who: the people of the space are listed and selected. Unselect anyone who should not be on duty. The order of the list is the duty order.
  • When it changes hands: daily, weekly or every two weeks, the day and the time of the handoff, and the team’s timezone (type a city to search).
  • Language: the language of DutyDesk’s cards and messages in this space, for everyone in it. English, Español, Français, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Italiano or 日本語. A new space starts in the Google language of whoever added DutyDesk.

Choose Save rotation. DutyDesk posts who is on duty and who is next. From then on, it posts a handoff message in the space at each handoff time and notifies the next person.

To change the rotation later, run /dd_setup again or choose Edit rotation on the /dd_duty card.

Send a request

Mention DutyDesk with your request in the space:

@DutyDesk the VPN is down for the Paris office

DutyDesk replies in the thread of your message with a request card and notifies the person on duty. On the card:

  • Claim: you are on it. Everyone in the space sees who took it.
  • Reassign: give it to someone else in the rotation. They are notified.
  • Resolve: done. The card shows who resolved it.

Nobody claims it? After 30 minutes DutyDesk mentions the person on duty again in the thread. After 60 minutes the request goes to the next person in the rotation, once. Change the delay, or turn it off, in /dd_setup › Requests nobody claims (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or Never).

/dd_requests lists the open requests and the ones resolved during the current shift. The next handoff message says how many requests are still open, so nothing is lost between shifts.

Skip or swap a shift

From the /dd_duty card:

  • Skip me next time: you are passed over at your next turn, once.
  • Swap the next shift: the person on duty or the next one can give the next shift to someone else in the rotation. The space is told who takes it, and both people are notified. One swap can be planned at a time.

For weekly and every-two-weeks rotations, DutyDesk posts a heads-up the day before each handoff, mentioning the next person, with Skip and Swap on the card. There is still time to change plans.

Away dates and covers

  • I’m away… (on the /dd_duty card): enter the first and the last day away, for yourself or a teammate. At every handoff whose shift touches those days, that person is passed over, then comes back in the usual order. The dates are listed on the /dd_duty card, each with Remove.
  • Cover a shift…: someone takes the duty for a few hours or days, without changing the rotation. Requests made in that window go to the person covering, and the space is told. Times are in the team’s timezone.

Rotation in your calendar

On the /dd_duty card, choose Add to calendar. DutyDesk shows, only to you, a private link to the rotation as a calendar feed. In Google Calendar, choose Other calendars › From URL and paste it: each shift shows as “On duty: name”, for the next 90 days, people away included. Google refreshes it every few hours.

Anyone with the link sees the names in the rotation. New link turns the old link off.

Side panel in Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Docs

DutyDesk also has a side panel in Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides. Click the DutyDesk icon in the bar on the right of the screen. The first time, Google asks you to let DutyDesk know who you are (your account and language); it gets no access to your emails, events or files.

For each Chat space with DutyDesk you are a member of:

  • Who is on duty now, until when, and who is next.
  • Open requests: the list, each one opening its thread in Chat.
  • Rotation: the same setup as /dd_setup (people, schedule, follow-up, language). Saving posts the handoff in the space.
  • New request: write it and send. DutyDesk posts it in the space and notifies the person on duty, as an @DutyDesk mention would.
  • Open in Chat.

The side panel uses the language of your spaces. It comes with the next update of DutyDesk on the Google Workspace Marketplace: if you don’t see the icon yet, it is on its way.

Commands

Command What it does
@DutyDesk <request> Sends a request to the person on duty
/dd_duty Who is on duty, the next handoffs, Skip and Swap (only you see it)
/dd_requests Open requests and the ones resolved this shift
/dd_setup Set up or change the rotation
/dd_help Commands, your plan and links to this page

Plans and trial

Each space gets a 21-day free trial, no payment card needed. Then one plan per space, priced by the people in its rotation:

Plan People in the rotation Monthly Yearly
Small up to 5 $19 $190
Team up to 20 $39 $390

Choose a plan from /dd_help › See plans. Payment is handled by Polar, which sends the receipts. If the trial ends without a plan, nothing is deleted: /dd_duty still answers, but new requests are not routed and no handoff is posted until the space subscribes. To cancel, use Manage subscription on the same card. Details in the terms.

Remove DutyDesk

Click the space name › Apps & integrations, then remove DutyDesk. The rotation is kept, so it comes back as it was if you add DutyDesk again. To have everything erased, write to support-dutydesk@futzs.com.

Troubleshooting

DutyDesk does not answer a message

DutyDesk only sees messages that mention it. Type @DutyDesk and pick it from the list so the mention turns into a chip, then write the request.

“No rotation yet”

The space has no rotation. Choose Set up the rotation (or run /dd_setup), save, and mention DutyDesk again.

Someone is missing from the setup dialog

The dialog lists the people of the space. Add the person to the space first, then open /dd_setup again.

The handoff came at the wrong time

Check the timezone in /dd_setup. Handoffs follow the team’s timezone, including daylight saving time changes.

Still stuck?

Write to support-dutydesk@futzs.com with the space name and what happened. A person reads every message.
