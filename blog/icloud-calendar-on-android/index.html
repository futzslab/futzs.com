How to see your iCloud calendar on Android

Serge Koudoro · 3 min read

Switched from iPhone to Android, or share a family calendar with someone who uses Android? Apple makes no Calendar app for Android, and Android has no iCloud account type. The way around it: copy the iCloud calendar into Google Calendar. Every Android phone shows Google Calendar.

Step 1: Create an app-specific password

Apple does not let other apps use your main Apple password.

  1. Sign in at account.apple.com.
  2. Open Sign-In and Security › App-Specific Passwords.
  3. Create one named ICalSync and copy it (xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx).

Two-factor authentication must be on. Step-by-step guide.

Step 2: Sync iCloud into Google Calendar

On a computer:

  1. Open Google Calendar and install ICalSync from the right-hand panel.
  2. Choose iCloud, enter your Apple ID and the app-specific password.
  3. Pick the iCloud calendar and the Google calendar it copies into, then click Sync.

ICalSync checks iCloud every few minutes and copies new, moved and deleted events.

Step 3: Turn the calendar on in Android

  1. Open the Google Calendar app on your phone.
  2. Tap ☰ › Settings, then your Google account.
  3. Tap the calendar and turn on Sync.

Your iCloud events now show on Android, in the Google Calendar app and in any calendar app that reads your Google account.

Free alternative: a public link

On iPhone: Calendar › Calendars › ⓘ next to the calendar › Public Calendar › Share Link. In Google Calendar on a computer: Other calendars › + › From URL, paste the link.

It costs nothing, but anyone with the link can read your events, and Google refreshes it only every few hours. Fine for a sports schedule, not for a personal calendar. Details in our full iCloud to Google guide.

Questions

Can I use iCloud Calendar on Android?

Not directly. Apple has no Calendar app for Android, and Android cannot add an iCloud account. Copy the iCloud calendar into Google Calendar instead: the Google Calendar app on your Android phone then shows it like any other calendar.

Why doesn't my iCloud calendar show on my Android phone?

If you added it in Google Calendar on a computer, open the Google Calendar app on Android, go to Settings, tap the calendar and turn on Sync. Calendars added from a URL can also take several hours to appear.

Do I need to make my iCloud calendar public?

No. A public link works, but anyone with it can read your events and Google refreshes it only every few hours. A sync with an Apple app-specific password keeps the calendar private and updates it within minutes.
